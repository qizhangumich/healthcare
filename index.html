§ 1.1Five questions五个问题
Why China?为什么是中国？

Scale and speed of discovery. China-sourced assets were 32% of global licensing deals in H1 2025, and 40% of deals by the top-20 pharma groups in 2025 involved Chinese assets. Phase I trials run in about 7 months against 17 or more in the US. Sourced有出处 研发规模与速度。2025年上半年，中国资产占全球授权交易的32%；2025年全球前20大药企的交易中有40%涉及中国资产。中国I期临床约需7个月，美国则需17个月以上。Sourced有出处

Why healthcare?为什么是医疗健康？

Gulf demand is structural: adult diabetes of 17–25% and adult obesity of 30–42%, with breast and colorectal cancer leading incidence. Saudi Arabia’s biotechnology strategy, Abu Dhabi’s HELM life-sciences cluster and Qatar’s national health strategy each make the sector a stated priority. Fact事实 海湾需求具有结构性：成人糖尿病患病率17–25%、成人肥胖率30–42%，乳腺癌与结直肠癌发病居前。沙特生物技术战略、阿布扎比HELM生命科学集群和卡塔尔国家卫生战略，均将该领域列为明确优先方向。Fact事实

Why now?为什么是现在？

Global pharma faces a patent cliff of about $300bn and is buying Chinese assets at record pace: roughly $110bn of headline value in H1 2026 alone. US rules are simultaneously making deal structure and investor domicile a live variable. Sourced有出处 全球药企面临约3,000亿美元专利悬崖，正以创纪录速度引进中国资产：仅2026年上半年交易总额就约1,100亿美元。与此同时，美国规则使交易结构和投资方注册地成为现实变量。Sourced有出处

Why the Gulf?为什么是海湾？

The public record already shows China–Gulf healthcare activity: licensing frameworks, technology transfer, R&D centres, hospital installations and sovereign participation in Chinese listings. Saudi Arabia adds market scale and codified localisation incentives. Fact事实 公开记录显示，中国与海湾的医疗合作已经发生：授权框架、技术转移、研发中心、医院装机，以及主权资本参与中国企业上市。沙特则提供市场规模和成文的本地化激励。Fact事实

§ 1.2Five findings五项发现

China’s rise is structural, not cyclical中国的崛起是结构性的，而非周期性的

Headline licensing value rose from $47.5bn to $135.6bn in a year, NMPA approvals hit a record, and FDA-approved China-discovered drugs now include zanubrutinib, toripalimab, fruquintinib, penpulimab and sunvozertinib. Sourced有出处 授权交易总额一年内由475亿美元升至1,356亿美元，国家药监局审批创新高；获FDA批准的中国原研药已包括泽布替尼、特瑞普利单抗、呋喹替尼、派安普利单抗和舒沃替尼。Sourced有出处

Headline values overstate cash交易总额远高于实际现金

Upfronts were 5.2% of headline value in 2025 and 5.8% in H1 2026. Across May–October 2026 deal tables, disclosed upfronts total about $6.5bn against about $99bn of headline value (our sum). Underwrite on near-term cash, not milestones. Sourced有出处 Inference推断 首付款占2025年交易总额的5.2%、2026年上半年的5.8%。在2026年5–10月的交易表中，已披露首付款合计约65亿美元，总额约990亿美元（本报告加总）。估值应基于近期现金，而非里程碑。Sourced有出处 Inference推断

Regulatory reliance is the main gate监管依赖路径是主要门槛

Saudi reliance pathways name FDA and EMA, including in guidance effective January 2027. We found no public UAE document listing China’s NMPA as a reference authority; Qatar and Oman remain unverified. Assets with FDA or EMA approval, and CE- or FDA-cleared devices, are therefore the most Gulf-ready. Fact事实 Inference推断 沙特依赖性审评路径明确指向FDA与EMA，2027年1月生效的新指南亦然。我们未找到将中国国家药监局列为参照机构的阿联酋公开文件；卡塔尔和阿曼尚未核实。因此，已获FDA或EMA批准的药品，以及获CE或FDA认证的器械，最具进入海湾的条件。Fact事实 Inference推断

The white space is regional rights, localisation and medtech空白机会在区域权益、本地化和医疗器械

Large licences usually hand worldwide ex-China rights to the buyer. The actionable space is un-partnered assets, biosimilar and vaccine technology transfer, as in ’s 2026 aflibercept biosimilar licence to Saudi SVAX, and medtech companies already selling abroad, such as (53% of revenue overseas). Inference推断 大型授权通常把除中国外的全球权益交给买方。可执行空间在于尚未对外授权的资产、生物类似药与疫苗技术转移（如2026年向沙特SVAX授权阿柏西普生物类似药），以及已在海外销售的器械企业（如，海外收入占53%）。Inference推断

Deal structure is now a regulatory variable交易结构已成为监管变量

The US BIOSECURE Act became law on 18 December 2025. A proposed Treasury rule of 26 October 2026 targets US investment in Chinese biotech while largely sparing plain licences. Non-US vehicles are not directly bound, but anti-evasion terms and US co-investors require specialist legal review. Fact事实 Inference推断 美国《生物安全法》于2025年12月18日成为法律。美国财政部2026年10月26日的拟议规则针对美国资本投资中国生物科技，但基本豁免纯授权交易。非美国载体不直接受其约束，但反规避条款及美国共同投资人的存在，要求进行专业法律审查。Fact事实 Inference推断

§ 2.1Licensing economics授权经济学

Out-licensing has become the main way Chinese biotech turns science into cash. In 2025, upfront payments of about $7.0bn exceeded all private financing of Chinese innovative-drug companies, about $5.2bn, for the first time. Multinationals paid 69% of those upfronts, and assets at Phase II or later took 65%. Sourced有出处 对外授权已成为中国生物科技把科学转化为现金的主要途径。2025年，约70亿美元首付款首次超过中国创新药企业一级市场融资总额（约52亿美元）。跨国药企支付了其中69%，II期及以后阶段资产获得65%。Sourced有出处
